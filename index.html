Welcome to the Omaha History Explorer

Explore the history of Omaha's neighborhoods on a 3D map.

  • Read a story: click an orange point, or choose a story from the Local Stories list.
  • Move: drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom.
  • Tilt and rotate: right-drag, or use two fingers on touch screens.
  • Add maps: use the checkboxes in the Layers panel.
  • Lost? Press "Reset View".
